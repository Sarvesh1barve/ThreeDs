THREE D'S — PERSONAL STUDIO WEBSITE PROTOTYPE
=============================================

MAIN FILE
  three_ds_personal_studio_prototype.html

QUICK START
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open three_ds_personal_studio_prototype.html with Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  3. Scroll slowly through the "From a thought to a thing" printing animation.
  4. Try the lamp light toggle, product categories, product quick views,
     add-to-bag animations, nameplate personalizer and custom-print brief.
  5. Use the floating "Logo & colours" button at bottom left to select the
     ORIGINAL Three D's logo and adjust the primary brand accent colour.

DESIGN DIRECTION
  Friendly boutique studio, not a cold manufacturing catalogue.
  Core idea: 'Little things. Big feelings.'
  Illustrated 3D-print objects, soft lavender and ivory, organic detailing,
  gallery-like product cards and intimate gift-led storytelling.

INSTAGRAM / BRAND ASSET LIMITATION
  The official Instagram profile https://www.instagram.com/threeds_india/
  is inaccessible to automated retrieval in this environment. Thus the
  original trademark/logo and exact brand colours cannot be verified.
  The page uses a provisional badge by default, may attempt to display a
  public profile avatar in browsers where it is available, and supports
  uploading the genuine brand logo in the local UI. Uploading a logo
  replaces the placeholder and suggests an accent colour locally.
  No assertion is made that the placeholder is the actual Three D's logo.

DEMO FUNCTIONALITY
  - Desktop and mobile layouts; smooth reveal and hover transitions.
  - 6 illustrated products with category filters.
  - Interactive bag: add, quantity, removal, subtotal and local persistence.
  - Sample product detail modal.
  - Scroll-driven FDM printer simulation, with a smoother eased transition.
  - Nameplate personalization by name, recipient and colour.
  - Custom 3D printing brief; accepts a local file selection; no uploads.
  - Instagram links to the real account.
  - Logo and primary-colour customization with local persistence.

NOT A LIVE SHOP
  Product concepts and prices are illustrative, not sourced from the
  Three D's catalogue. There are no payments, inventory or actual orders.
  No data is uploaded from the custom-print demo. Contact details,
  real catalogue, genuine brand assets, checkout integrations and product
  photography must be supplied before any production launch.

TECHNOLOGY
  One standalone HTML file with inline CSS, JS and self-contained vector
  illustrations. Optional Google font and public avatar requests enhance
  appearance where available. No server, build step, or API key required.
